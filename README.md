### ALGO-000097: Graph isomorphism FPT in maximum degree
- **Subfield:** Parameterized algorithms, kernelization, and structural graph algorithms
- **Open:** Test isomorphism of maximum-degree-\(d\) graphs in \(f(d)n^{O(1)}\) time.
- **Status:** Algorithms with \(n^{\mathrm{polylog}(d)}\)-type running time are known. Moving all degree dependence out of the exponent of \(n\) remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would give substantially stronger guarantees for bounded-valence structures.
- **Key works:** Luks (1982), *Isomorphism of Graphs of Bounded Valence Can Be Tested in Polynomial Time*; Grohe, Neuen, and Schweitzer, *A Faster Isomorphism Test for Graphs of Small Degree*, arXiv:1802.04659, DOI: 10.1137/19M1245293.
